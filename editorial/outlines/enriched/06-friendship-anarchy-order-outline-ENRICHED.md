## CRITICAL TERMINOLOGY: SURPLUS vs. EXCESS

**This chapter seeds the Obligation to Excess (fully developed in Ch 9). The distinction is CRUCIAL:**

| Term | Definition | Character | What It's For |
|------|------------|-----------|---------------|
| **Surplus** | Buffer beyond necessities | Security + Growth | Reinvestment, savings, insurance against bad seasons, buying better tools |
| **Excess** | Capacity beyond what can be sold or is needed for security | Freedom + Generosity | Can be risked on discovery OR given through generosity without threatening survival or security |

**The Key Insight for Chapter 6:**
- Markets generate **surplus** (the "four thousand nails" that build buffer)
- Surplus accumulates into **excess** (the "thousand nails nobody strictly needs")
- **Friendship is where EXCESS gets deployed through voluntary generosity**
- Do NOT conflate excess with reserves/insurance (that's surplus)
- Do NOT suggest friends sacrifice necessities—they deploy EXCESS

**The Flow (must be clear in generation):**
```
Excellence → Surplus (buffer/growth) → Excess (freedom) → Friendship (voluntary channel for generosity)
```
